# Research scope and contribution discussion

These are personal learning projects developed with AI assistance. They are not peer-reviewed publications, industrial deployments, or evidence of chip-design experience. AI assistance does not substitute for verifying code, data splits, metrics, and conclusions.

The notebooks document the implemented workflow and saved results. For a supervisor discussion, distinguish project-level functionality from the steps personally designed, implemented, debugged, or independently verified. No finer-grained ownership claim is asserted by this publication preparation.

Next research steps are proposals, not completed work: prospective validation, stronger baselines, calibrated uncertainty, and hardware-aware evaluation where suitable. No RTL, FPGA deployment, quantization, measured energy reduction, or EDA flow has been demonstrated here.
